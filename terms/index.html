1. What Entwined does

Entwined lets two partners share selected calendar events with each other while each continues to use their own Apple or Google calendars. The app reads calendars on your device, uploads limited details of events from calendars you choose to share, and writes your partner's shared events into dedicated calendars on your device ("Entwined: Together" and "Entwined: Partner"). It can also group events into an "Our Time Together" block based on keywords. Our Privacy Policy explains what data is handled and how.
