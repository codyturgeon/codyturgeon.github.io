Entwined Support

Need help with Entwined: Shared Calendars? Email entwinedcalendars.support@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: in the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This deletes your shared events, push tokens, couple membership and profile from our servers and removes the Entwined calendars from your phone. It does not cancel a subscription.
  • Unpair from my partner: open Settings and tap Unpair. Your uploaded events are deleted from our servers and your partner's events are removed from your Entwined calendars.
  • Cancel a subscription: in the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Entwined. Deleting the app or your Entwined account does not cancel it.
  • Restore purchases: open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with. One subscription covers both partners.
  • Pricing: $2.99 per month or $24.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Privacy and terms: see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
