Privacy Policy — Entwined: Shared Calendars

Effective date: October 10, 2026 Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the iOS app Entwined: Shared Calendars ("Entwined", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by Cody Turgeon ("we", "us").

  • Bundle ID: com.codyturgeon.entwinedcalendars
  • Contact: entwinedcalendars.support@gmail.com

The short version

  • Entwined lets two partners see each other's chosen calendar events while each keeps using their own Apple or Google calendars.
  • The app reads your calendar on your device. You pick which calendars to share.
  • From the calendars you pick, only each event's title, start time, end time, all-day flag, and a stable key derived by the app are uploaded. Notes, locations, and attendees are never uploaded.
  • We do not show ads, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its data from Settings inside the app.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Calendar information (only from calendars you choose to share)

With your permission, the app uses Apple's EventKit framework to read calendar events stored on your iPhone. This can include calendars you have added to the iOS Calendar app, such as Google calendars. Reading happens on your device.

For the calendars you select to share, the app uploads the following for each event:

  • Event title
  • Start date/time
  • End date/time
  • Whether it is an all-day event
  • A stable key the app derives so it can recognize the same event across syncs

The app does not upload event notes, locations, or attendees. Events from calendars you have not selected are not uploaded.

Please keep in mind that event titles can contain personal details (for example, "Doctor appointment"). Any event title in a calendar you share will be visible to your paired partner.

1.2 Account and pairing information

  • Account identifier. The app signs you in anonymously, which creates a user ID in our backend without asking for your name or email.
  • Profile. We store no profile fields beyond your account identifier and when it was created.
  • Couple membership. When you pair with your partner using an invite code, we store the link between your two accounts so each of you can see the other's shared events.

1.3 Push token

The app uses silent background push notifications only to tell your device that new shared events are ready to sync. To do this, we store your device's push token in our backend. These notifications do not display messages to you.

1.4 Subscription information

Entwined offers a subscription ($2.99/month or $24.99/year, or the local equivalent shown in the App Store, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers; one subscription covers both partners of a paired couple) purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system and managed with RevenueCat. We receive information about your subscription status (for example, whether it is active and when it renews or expires) and purchase history tied to your app account identifier. To give your partner the benefit of a subscription you bought, we store the date until which your subscription is active and make it visible to your partner's account (only that date, not payment details). We do not receive your payment card details; Apple handles payment.

1.5 Information that stays on your device

  • Your custom keywords for the "Our Time Together" feature and any manual overrides you make are stored only on your device (in local app storage). They are not uploaded to us.
  • Calendar events from calendars you have not chosen to share stay on your device.

1.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier, or browsing history, and the app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the app's features:

  • To show each partner the other's shared events.
  • To write your partner's shared events into dedicated calendars on your device named "Entwined: Together" and "Entwined: Partner".
  • To group events into the green "Our Time Together" block. The app does this using a keyword rule (built-in words such as trip, date, vacation, anniversary, and flight, plus any keywords you add). Your custom keywords and overrides are applied on your device.
  • To send silent push notifications that trigger a sync.
  • To check and manage your subscription.
  • To respond to you if you contact us for support.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent your information.

3. Calendars the app writes to your device

To show your partner's shared events, the app creates and writes to two calendars on your device: "Entwined: Together" and "Entwined: Partner". The app writes to these dedicated calendars rather than editing your own calendars. Depending on how your device is set up, iOS may sync calendars created on the device to an account such as iCloud. The app creates them in an on-device calendar source if one exists, otherwise in the account of your default calendar (usually iCloud), in which case iOS may sync them across your devices. When you unpair or delete your account, the app removes the mirrored events and these two calendars from your device.

Your partner will see the event fields listed in Section 1.1 for any calendar you choose to share.

We may also disclose information if required by law or valid legal process.

5. Security

Data in Supabase is protected with row-level security rules designed so that only you and your paired partner can access your couple's shared events. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Data is sent between the app and our backend over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Where your information is stored

Data stored in our backend is hosted in the United States (AWS us-east-1, via Supabase). If you use the app from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and shared data while your account exists. Shared events are updated as your selected calendars change. When you stop sharing a calendar, or events are removed or moved outside the shared time window, the corresponding events are removed from our backend at your next sync. When you unpair or delete your account, your uploaded events are deleted from our backend. Backups kept by our hosting provider may persist for a limited time under its own retention practices.

8. Your choices

  • Choose what to share. You can change which calendars are shared at any time in the app.
  • Calendar permission. You can turn off calendar access in iOS Settings. Without it, the app cannot read or sync your events.
  • Subscription. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account deletes your shared events, push tokens, couple membership, profile, and authentication account from our backend. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings. When you delete your account or unpair, your partner stops seeing your shared events at their next sync, and the app removes the mirrored events and the "Entwined: Together" / "Entwined: Partner" calendars from your own device. Purchase and subscription records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat for accounting and legal purposes, and are not deleted when you delete your account.
  • Local data. Custom keywords and overrides are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Questions or requests. Email entwinedcalendars.support@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information.

9. Children

Entwined is designed for couples and is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old to use the app. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
